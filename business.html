Common questions

Is PlushLife free?

Yes. The current beta remains free. The future pricing above is only a proposal.

What is a Guardian?

A Guardian is a trusted supporter, such as a friend, partner, parent or caregiver. You're the Cozy, and you're always in control of what you share.

Will my current features disappear?

No feature is being removed by this preview. Any future plan change should be announced clearly before it happens.

Is PlushLife a medical app?

No. PlushLife is a routine and self-care companion, not a clinical treatment, medical device or emergency service.
