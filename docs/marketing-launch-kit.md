# PlushLife organic launch kit
Updated 2026-11-03. **Prepared content, not published social posts or an activated Google Play production listing.** No paid campaigns, automated DMs, tracking SDKs, or collection of private task/journal/Guardian data.

## Campaign objective
Help the first 20–30 **consenting** testers discover the existing web app, create a first task, and give honest feedback. The Android app is in **closed testing**; no one should be told it is publicly downloadable without a valid invited-tester path. We have no verified install, conversion, retention, or subscriber baseline yet.

## Primary destinations
- Web app: https://sablewolphen.github.io/plushlist/ (verify after deployment; it may ask users to sign in).
- Explanation and beta feedback page: https://sablewolphen.github.io/plushlist/business.html (verify after deployment).
- Beta interest: use the page's user-initiated email link. This **is not** a connected newsletter or signup backend. Do not claim a successful signup until the owner replies and qualifies them.
- Android closed-testing opt-in: **not yet confirmed**. Before promoting Android, retrieve the real opt-in/store URL from Play Console and ensure the target person is eligible. Never invent a Play link.

## Positioning tests
A. "One little step is enough." — gentle productivity without pressure.
B. "Pick a Tiny, Soft, Full, or Rest day." — adapt the day to available energy.
C. "A little help, on your terms." — optional, consent-based Guardian support.

Start with A; test one angle at a time using direct feedback, not inferred tracking.

## Operating campaign (no paid spend)
Week 1: Confirm the real landing URL and a synthetic demo account. Invite 5–10 people personally who have **consented** to provide feedback. Ask them to complete first-task onboarding and note failures; avoid public health-targeted ads.
Week 2: Make 3 short screen recordings of actual UI with private data replaced by synthetic examples. Post only where group rules permit self-promotion, with a clear affiliation disclosure.
Week 3: Refresh Google Play screenshot candidates using real builds, then use Play Console's native store listing experiment tools **after** the target listing is available; change one asset per experiment and compare acquisition/retention.
Week 4: Review feedback and baseline metrics, fix the biggest activation blocker, and repeat the strongest compliant channel.

## Operational controls
- No scraped emails, purchased followers, unsolicited bulk DMs, personal-data sharing, or audience profiling based on sensitive traits.
- Never claim paid subscribers or proven retention before backed by actual data.
- No inferred conversion rates from page traffic until measurement is enabled, approved, and sourced.
- Follow community rules, use disclosure, and ask before emailing individuals.
- Do not spend money or publicly post without verifying the destination and content.
- The beta-interest email is a manual inbound contact, not a database or mailing list.

## KPIs to review weekly
Eligible invites sent (with consent); genuine visits or click counts only if verifiable; people who opened web beta; first-task completions; D7 returners among eligible cohorts; feedback themes; test crashes and failed saves. Keep denominators and observation dates; unknown stays unknown.
